How to drive

  • Left pad — slide left/right to steer
  • GAS to go · BRAKE to stop (hold it to back up) · Horn for fun
  • Only the Amphi Cruiser can drive into the pond, the pool and the creek. Hit the jump ramp in the big field!
  • Keyboard: arrow keys — ← / → steer · ↑ gas · ↓ brake · H or Space horn · W / S raise / lower the Kubota's loader, plow or the Bobcat's lift arm · H horn
  • Zero-turn: push both lap bars forward to go, push one further to turn, pull them back to reverse, and push one forward with the other back to spin in place. The throttle toggles between 🐢 LOW and 🐇 HIGH (keys: ↑/↓ both bars, ←/→ turn or spin, T throttle).
  • MGB stick shift: drag the knob through the H-pattern (or Shift to shift up, Ctrl to shift down, or keys 1–4, R, N). Start in 1st and shift up as it revs. Tap Manual to switch to automatic. Tap Top up (or T) to raise or lower the convertible top.
